Methodology

Annual Monte Carlo. Before FIRE, savings are added. After FIRE, spending is withdrawn. Pension begins at pension age and offsets withdrawals after flat tax. Pension accrual is entered as additional monthly pension earned per working year. The earliest FIRE year is the first integer year where non-ruin probability reaches the target confidence. The same random return paths are reused across candidate FIRE years to reduce noise. Finnish preset pension accrual is estimated as annual earnings × 1.5% ÷ 12, expressed as extra monthly pension earned per working year.
